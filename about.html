About Me!

To all who do not know me, my name is Yasmine Brown, the founder of YAZBFITNESS. From before I can remember, I have always had a love for sport and exercise. Growing up I was a competitive figure skater and later went to study Applied Sport and Exercise Science at Robert Gordons University where I graduated in 2026. I gained my Level 2 fitness instructor qualification, Level 3 mat pilates alongside my Level 3 personal training and decided it was officially time to share my passion. I love to motivate others especially through current group classes and have finally taken the plunge to extend this love to 1-1 training.

As a girl who loves a yap, I want the sessions to allow individuals to build confidence, trust and positive relationships and encourage you to be the best version possible of yourself. We constantly limit our abilities and it is my goal to show everyone just how capable we all are and of course, most of all, be the healthiest, strongest humans possible!!

So if this sounds like something that resonates with you, drop me an email or complete the online enquiry form and let’s schedule in a wee chat! The sooner you start, the sooner you shine!

Love always, Yaz x
