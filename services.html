1-to-1 Personal Training

Enjoy fully personalised coaching with one-to-one training sessions tailored to your goals, fitness level, and experience. Each session is delivered at your scheduled appointment time, providing expert guidance, accountability, and ongoing support. Whether you're new to the gym, looking to build confidence, or wanting to take your training to the next level, you'll receive individual attention to help you improve your technique, stay motivated, and achieve lasting results.

Buddy Personal Training

Train alongside a friend, partner, or family member while benefiting from personalised coaching. Buddy Personal Training follows the same structured approach as one-to-one sessions, with workouts tailored to suit both participants. You'll share a scheduled appointment, motivate each other, and enjoy a fun, supportive environment while working towards your individual fitness goals.

Group Personal Training

Group Personal Training is ideal for groups of three or more who want to train together in a motivating and energetic setting. Sessions are designed to challenge everyone while accommodating different fitness levels, helping you build strength, improve fitness, and stay accountable as part of a supportive team.

Online Coaching

Achieve your fitness goals wherever you are with personalised online coaching. You'll receive a customised training programme, weekly workout guidance, and ongoing support to keep you progressing. Whether you have a busy schedule, travel frequently, or prefer to train independently, online coaching offers the flexibility to work out on your own time while still benefiting from professional advice, regular check-ins, and continuous support whenever you need it.
